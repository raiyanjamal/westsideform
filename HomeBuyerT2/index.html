Cancellation Policy

We understand situations arise when you may need to cancel or reschedule your survey. To do so, we request you notify us at least 48 hours in advance. This allows us to offer your appointment slot to another client on our waiting list.

Cancellations or rescheduling requests made less than 48 hours before your survey may be subject to a cancellation fee of £150.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation with this policy, as it helps us maintain efficient scheduling and service quality for all our clients. In case of emergencies or unforeseen circumstances, please contact us directly to discuss your situation.
